# Product Context: Khairul Faridz Portfolio

## Overview
A high-craft personal developer portfolio for Khairul Faridz, a student full-stack software engineer and creative builder based in Indonesia (West Java).

## Core Objective
Showcase technical capabilities, production-ready web and data engineering projects, and a disciplined approach to software craft with an editorial minimalist aesthetic derived from the Stitch design system.

## Audience
- Tech leads, engineering managers, and recruiters seeking talented full-stack developers (React/Next.js/TypeScript/Laravel/PostgreSQL/Databricks).
- Collaborators and clients looking for modern, responsive digital products with strong visual polish and clean architecture.

## Personality & Tone
- **Mode:** Experience / Persuade (high craft, deliberate typography, editorial restraint, no generic AI tropes).
- **Core Principle:** "Consistency as an engineering principle" — disciplined engineering paired with high design sensitivity.
- **Copy Voice:** Direct, confident, technical, unpadded.

## Primary Sections
1. **Hero:** Opening statement, edition, discipline, and quick direct channels.
2. **About Me:** Professional background, focus, philosophy, and milestone journey timeline.
3. **Selected Works:** Detailed interactive showcases for real-world projects (CSV Template Validation, LifeSync, CritiPlay) highlighting system architecture and contributions.
4. **Tech Stack:** 12 targeted capabilities across frontend, backend, database, and lakehouse tooling.
5. **Beyond The Code:** Interactive circular lens reveal connecting physical discipline (gym/fitness) to software resilience.
6. **Social Media / Connect:** Direct inquiries, email contact, social platforms, and work availability status.
7. **Certificates & Achievements:** Minimalist intentional empty state acknowledging future milestones without padding.
